New: two dropdowns under the title, swim length and travel. With nothing chosen, the list shows every open pool, sorted by distance. Once you choose something, the dropdowns turn white and the pools are calculated for it. Without a location, or far from Berlin, both dropdowns are hidden and pools are sorted by opening hours. Settings are tidied up (My Swim, Only pools with …, More options), and the shortest swim length is 15 minutes. Please check in particular: the two dropdowns on iPad and the folded iPhone Duo, Settings, and the map when you are not in Berlin.
